Machu Picchu

Machu Picchu located in Cuzco Peru

Machu Picchu is an awe-inspiring 15th-century Inca citadel perfectly cradled atop a misty mountain ridge in the Peruvian Andes. As a brilliant testament to the architectural mastery of the Inca Empire, this UNESCO World Heritage site fascinates global travelers with its sophisticated stone terraces, sacred temples, and mysterious cosmic alignments.

Things to Do

  • Trek the Classic Inca Trail: Embark on an epic multi-day hiking journey through diverse ecological zones and ancient stone paths that lead directly to the Sun Gate.
  • Explore the Main Citadel: Take a guided walking tour through the archaeological site to uncover the secrets of the Temple of the Sun, the Intihuatana stone, and the Sacred Rock
  • Hike Huayna Picchu: Climb the steep, dramatic peak towering directly behind the ruins for an exhilarating aerial view of the entire ancient complex.
