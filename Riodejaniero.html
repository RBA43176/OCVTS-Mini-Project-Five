Rio de Janiero

Rio de Janiero located in Brazil

Rio de Janeiro is a vibrant coastal metropolis in Brazil renowned for its dramatic landscapes, lush rainforests, and world-famous golden beaches.
Framed by the iconic silhouette of Sugarloaf Mountain and the festive energy of Copacabana, the city perfectly unites rich cultural traditions like samba with a breathtaking natural playground.

Things to Do

  • Visit Christ the Redeemer: Take a train or hike up Corcovado Mountain to stand beneath the colossal Art Deco statue and absorb panoramic views of the city.
  • Ride the Sugarloaf Cable Car: Journey across two glass-walled cable car lines to reach the summit of Sugarloaf Mountain for a stunning 360-degree sunset over Guanabara Bay.
  • Climb the Escadaria Selarón: Marvel at the vibrant, world-famous public staircase completely adorned in a mosaic of thousands of colorful handmade tiles.
